🛒 Shop & Moon Pearls

Moon Pearls

Moon Pearls are earned, never required: 1 pearl per dawn survived, plus quest rewards, find-set turn-ins and monster loot. They buy keepers (Dot 150, Skip 200), pearl-priced looks and the helper pets. Nothing that keeps you alive is sold for pearls or Robux — survival is always earned.
