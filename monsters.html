🦀 Monsters

Every monster can be beaten, and every monster drops loot — the tougher the monster, the better the tier, and loot grows +25% every 10 nights (up to double). Monsters never take more than 1 heart per hit.

🌊 Old Tidemaw

The oldest thing in the sea. Tidemaw rises first on night 10, then every 3–5 nights at random — the morning before, the sea goes glassy and still and Marlo warns you. Don't follow its golden lure (it tugs swimmers toward it and snaps at 6 studs: 1 heart and you're flung home). Waves roll in about every 30 seconds, each costing the lamp 8 oil and shrinking the light. To drive it off, light the Three Fires — Elias's old signal fires on the beach. Driving it off pays pearls, coins, supplies and a shot at the Tidemaw's Treasures find set (its Scale, Tooth and Lure-Light Pearl).

🌿 Mossback, the big neighbor

Mossback lives in the jungle cave. He charges and throws — hearts stay at 1 per hit. But Mossback remembers how you treat him:

  • Feed him fish. He'll sit and munch for 12 seconds. Every fish resets the timer — and every 3 fish shared, ever, he tells you one part of his 5-part story (talk to him while he sits). Hear all five to earn the title Friend of Mossback.
  • Friends are safe. Anyone who has shared 3+ fish is a friend: he never charges or throws at them.
  • Grudges are real. Each night he comes out and nobody shares a fish, he holds a bigger grudge (up to 3): he warns less, charges faster, and from grudge 2 takes one more hit to shoo. Sharing a fish takes a grudge off.

Monsters get tougher

As nights pass: Fog Crabs take +1 flip every 8 nights (up to 5), Brutes take +1 hit every 10 nights (up to 6), Tidemaw's waves come sooner each visit, and Mossback shows up more often after night 12. A dusk notice tells you when something has gotten tougher.
