📜 Quests

  • Daily & weekly quests on the quest board (island, and tucked beside Marlo's stall on the dock): survive, build, fish, flip crabs, and dock games — the harbor bell chain, the ring race (including a weekly "finish under 30 seconds"), the crab derby, postcard photos and dock fishing. At most one dock daily per day.
  • Tide quests — a rotating bonus quest worth 3–4 pearls that changes every 4 hours, with a banner when a new one rolls in.
  • Night quests (night 10+): light Elias's signal fire; drive off Old Tidemaw.
  • New quests are marked with red NEW tags, "!" dots on the Quests and Menu buttons, and a bobbing ❗ over the board — so you never miss one.
  • Streaks: the "Most Nights in a Row" board counts dawns you survived across runs — it never resets on a failed night; spirit nights just don't add. "Nights Survived" is a lifetime total.
