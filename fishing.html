🎣 Fishing

How fishing works

  • Cast at a fish school from a raft or boat, off the island pier, or at the Crew Dock. The fish is picked the moment you cast — its rarity decides the fight.
  • Wait through the nibble fake-outs. Reeling early just wastes the moment now — the fish stays.
  • When the ❗ BITE! ❗ hits, press REEL fast (big button on phone, Space/click on PC, R2/A on controller).
  • The fight: hold to pull the hook right, let go to drift left. Keep the hook on the darting fish to fill the meter. Rarer fish have a smaller zone, faster darts and a shorter hook window. "Something big!" means exactly that.
  • Pier casts always bite (3–8 s); school casts bite in 1.5–4 s. Rain makes fish bite 30% sooner.

Luck

Your odds of rare fish grow with: a better rod (Sturdy +0.3, Sea-Glass +0.7), night fishing (+0.15), rain or storm (+0.25), and lucky schools — about a quarter of schools sparkle gold (+0.6). Finn gets a wider catch zone; Calm Mode makes fights gentler.
