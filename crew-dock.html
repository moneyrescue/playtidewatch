⚓ The Crew Dock

The dock is the sunset harbor where every visit starts: pick your keeper, visit Marlo's stall, check the leaderboards, and sail out with your crew. The first time you ever visit, the island calls you after 8 seconds — after that, never; look around all you like. Boarding a raft takes standing aboard for 2.5 seconds, with a 5-second last call to step off.

Things to do

  • The harbor bell — ring it 7 times, each within 3 seconds of the last (friends can help), for confetti.
  • Ring race — paddle a raft through 10 buoy gates in order from the west race jetty. Server-timed; the jetty board keeps the fastest times.
  • Crab derby — pick Pinchy, Sir Scuttles, Bubbles or Captain Sandy on the east shore. The race starts 15 seconds after the first pick.
  • Photo spot — the "Greetings from Tidewatch!" postcard frame. Step on the blue mat, or use the self-timer (5 s), strike a pose, and save the shot.
  • Fishing — the dock hands you a rod per cast; every cast bites.
  • Shell hunt, marshmallow roasting, sandcastles — and the shipwreck playground, the Leaky Lark.

🗝️ The Leaky Lark treasure hunt

Five trinkets are hidden in and around the wreck, saved forever once found (+1 Moon Pearl each; all five: +10 more and the title Captain of the Leaky Lark): the Silver Spoon (peek in the barrel — three times), the Brass Compass (pry open the crate), the Captain's Last Letter (open the hatch), the Gold Doubloon (take the crow's-nest lookout, follow the spyglass, dig by the anchor), and the Lucky Hat Feather (walk the plank — three times). The ship's wheel hints at the next trinket you haven't found.

Friends & Crew

The Friends & Crew button lists your Roblox friends: Join a friend's island or Invite them to yours with a reserved-server code. Three boarding rafts (Red, Blue, Green) sail independently — anyone aboard can tap Friends only by the mast so only friends of someone aboard may board; a raft unlocks when it empties.
