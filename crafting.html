🔨 Crafting & Tools

Crafted tools are yours forever — once you craft a tool you keep it across runs. Tier 2 tools unlock on night 3 of a run, tier 3 on night 6 (rods: night 5 and night 9). The Log Raft and Fishing Rod are never locked — craft them on day 1.
