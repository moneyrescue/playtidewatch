🎒 Backpacks grow with you

Your bag size follows the best night you've ever reached — it never shrinks. Bigger bags scale your supply, food and Pebble caps; Kelp Wraps always cap at 3.

🐷 Piglets

Three wild piglets live on the island. Play the befriend game, feed one to 5 hearts, give it a (filtered) name, and teach it 5 tricks — a befriended piglet is saved forever and can fetch for you.
