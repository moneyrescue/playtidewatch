🧱 Building & Your Camp

Build mode places fort pieces that persist with your crew's run. Basic pieces (walls, sand walls, torches, campfire, benches, flags) are open from the start; the rest unlock at Chapter 2 or night 2 of a run (Stone Wall waits for night 4). Bea the Builder pays 25% less for every piece.

Crew Chest (1 per crew)5 Driftwood, 4 Shells, 2 Sea GlassShared storage: up to 60 each of food, Pebbles, supplies and Kelp Wraps. Upgrades at the chest: Iron Lock (6 Shells, 3 Sea Glass — pirates rummage twice as long) and Hidden Cellar (4 Driftwood, 6 Shells — 5 of each kind can never be stolen).
Rest Hut (max 2)8 Driftwood, 4 Shells, 2 KelpHeal inside: +1 heart every 8 s (faster with a friend resting too). Make it cozy (4 Driftwood, 4 Kelp, 2 Shells) heals every 5 s and halves hunger drain inside. Faint and you wake here for half the oil, with 1 heart. Crabs can't walk in.
🏴‍☠️ Pirate raids (night 25+): a full Crew Chest glints. If your chest holds 20+ stealable things at dusk, pirates may raid (15% at 20 items, +1% per item, max 60%; never on Tidemaw or Calm nights). They hack through walls and shove (no hearts lost), grab an armful — at most 25% of what's stealable — and run for their ship. Bonk a loot-carrier and it drops the sack; carry it home to restore it. Loot that reaches the ship can still be recovered: reach the ship before dawn and hold Take back the loot.
